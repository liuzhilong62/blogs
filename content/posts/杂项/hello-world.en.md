## Why English?

After writing 80+ Chinese articles on PostgreSQL internals, case studies, and source code analysis, I'm launching an English section to share key insights with the global PostgreSQL community.

## What to Expect

I'll be translating selected articles from [the Chinese blog](https://lastdba.com) and occasionally writing original English content. Topics will include:

- PostgreSQL internals deep dives
- Production incident case studies
- Source code walkthroughs (vacuum, locking, WAL, planner)
- Paper reviews and academic database research

## Translation Policy

Each English article will link to its original Chinese version. I'm a native Chinese speaker, so please forgive any language rough edges — corrections and feedback are always welcome.

Stay tuned.
